# Privacy Policy for Site QA Auditor

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Site QA Auditor ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled by the Extension.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Site QA Auditor operates entirely within your local web browser.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** The Extension does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, IP address, or location).
- **Local Page Analysis:** Webpage content (DOM text, images, and anchor elements) is inspected locally in real-time solely to detect QA issues (Lorem Ipsum, broken # links, outdated copyright years, HTML heading structures) on pages you explicitly choose to audit.
- **No External Data Transmission:** Website content analyzed by the Extension is never sent to any external server or third party.

## 2. Local Browser Storage
The Extension uses `chrome.storage.local` strictly to store:
- User-configured scanner settings (target copyright year, category toggles).
- User-provided Figma API Personal Access Tokens (stored locally on your device).
- Cached audit reports keyed to open URLs to prevent unnecessary re-scanning when switching browser tabs.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
Site QA Auditor does not sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances.
